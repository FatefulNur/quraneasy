# Purity Before Reciting the Qur'an

Before a beginner picks up the mushaf, scholars have always emphasized a small set of habits that prepare both body and heart for recitation.

## Wudu and touching the mushaf

The majority view, drawn from the verse "None shall touch it except the purified" (Surah Al-Waqi'ah 56:79), is that one should be in a state of wudu when handling the mushaf. Reciting from memory without wudu is permitted, but wudu is still recommended out of respect.

## A clean place

A clean sitting place — free from filth and distraction — helps the heart settle. Bedrooms, prayer corners, or a quiet spot at home all work; what matters is calm and cleanliness.

## A clean mouth

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged the use of the miswak, especially before recitation, so that the mouth that carries the words of Allah is itself clean.

## What this slide skips

The slide intentionally stays short. Detailed rulings about wudu's conditions, masah on socks, and the differences between madhhabs belong in fiqh study — not in a beginner's first pass through the rules of tilawat.
